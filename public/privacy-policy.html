Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Welcome to EcoTrophy Innovations ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how our business management application (BillPro CRM / MyWaCa) collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you interact with us via our web application, WhatsApp Business messaging, Facebook Messenger, Facebook Lead Ads, or Instagram Direct.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a quotation, or submit an order enquiry, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Details: Full name, phone number, WhatsApp phone number, and email address.
  • Enquiry Details: Order requirements, product quantities, trophy specifications, event dates, and delivery location.
  • Social & Meta Channel Data: Sender ID, Facebook Profile Name, WhatsApp ID, and message contents sent to our official business channels.
  • Commercial Documents: Quotations, proforma invoices, invoices, and billing history.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Receiving, processing, and responding to your product enquiries and quotation requests.
  • Managing your orders through our production and delivery pipeline.
  • Sending status updates regarding your quotations, order production, and delivery tracking via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • Customer support and dispute resolution.

3. Data Protection and Storage

We implement industry-standard security protocols to protect your data. All database communications are encrypted in transit via SSL/TLS and stored securely in cloud infrastructure managed with strict role-based access controls.

4. User Data Deletion Request (GDPR / Meta Compliance)

We respect your right to control your personal data. You may request the permanent deletion of your data from our systems at any time by:

  • Sending an email with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" to support@ecotrophy.in or ecotrophystore@gmail.com, including your registered phone number or email address.
  • Sending a WhatsApp message stating "DELETE MY DATA" to our registered business WhatsApp number.

Upon receiving your request, your personal data will be completely deleted from our CRM database within 48 hours, and you will receive a confirmation message.

5. Third-Party Services

We interact with Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Cloud API, Facebook Messenger, and Instagram) in compliance with Meta Developer Policies and Terms of Service. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers.
